# 镜头表 · S4 — THE GATHERING (دورهمی گربه‌ها)

گربه‌ٔ سفید کوچک و راهنمای حنایی‌رنگ به گورستان قدیمی می‌رسند؛ صدها گربه در سکوت نشسته‌اند،
پیامبر سیامی روی صلیب سنگیِ نرم‌شده مثل موم نشسته است. شش نما، هر کدام ۸ ثانیه، جمعاً ۴۸ ثانیه.
بدون صدا، بدون نوشتهٔ روی تصویر.

画风：1930s surrealist oil painting in the language of Dalí — smooth blended brushwork, glowing
luminous paint, impossibly long soft shadows, thin horizon lines, cold night light. No outlines,
no 3D, no photoreal, no anime, no cartoon. Locked for the whole scene (and the film).

色板：`#101f3c` deep cobalt · `#1b2c50` night blue · `#c9d6e8` silver · `#e9eef6` moon white ·
`#3a3474` indigo mist · `#6c7a8c` stone grey · `#d99a3f` amber (the ONLY warm accent: eyes).
No bright reds, no saturated cartoon colours, no blue-purple gradient slop.

角色：frame library `thousand_cats/assets_characters/` — AI-painted RGBA layers, approved here in
S4 and reused unchanged in S5–S12 (`white_kitten.png`, `brown_tabby.png`, `siamese_prophet.png`).

**Checks against the template**

1. Opening is not a title card: frame one already holds the cross, the moon and the two cats; the
   last shot ends on a movement (the pull-back settling into the crowd of eyes), not a text card.
2. One look, one palette, one light for all six shots; every shot is the same cemetery, same night.
3. Shot length 8 s each — inside the 6–8 s envelope of the production prompt (Section 11), total 48 s
   (45–60 s).
4. On-screen text: **none** in all six shots (0 % text-led).
5. Adjacent shots change composition: wide / close push / ground-level two-shot / rows / medium /
   wide pull-back; the camera has a return (medium at 8–16, tilt out at 16–24, in on the stones at 24–32, close at 32–40, wide pull-back at 40–48).
