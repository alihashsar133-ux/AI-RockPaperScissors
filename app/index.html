How to Defend

🛡️

Your Duty

Enemies march from the desert toward the citadel gate (left). If they reach it, the gate loses integrity. At 0%, the city falls.

🏹

Shoot

Desktop: Move mouse to aim, click or SPACE to fire. W/S or ↑/↓ to move along the wall.
Mobile: Drag right side to aim, tap Fire. Use left pad to move.

🔥

Score & Combo

Chain hits without missing to build combo ×2.0, ×3.0… Headshots give +50. Every 5 waves you face a ram! Collect falling banners for heal & rapid fire.

Master the Defense

  • Hold the line: Stand on the wall ledges. Shift position to intercept different lanes.
  • Lead your shots: Enemies keep marching — aim slightly ahead.
  • Watch the banners: Gold banners = heal, Azure = 5-sec rapid volley.
  • Ram alert: When horns sound and dust rises, focus fire on the ram — it smashes the gate for 30 damage.
